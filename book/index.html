Namamicleans brand guidelinesBrand · 03

A drop, and the shine it leaves.

The drop is clean water. The gold curl inside it is the shine that's left when the job is done properly. The swirl between them is a Namamian at work.

Namamicleans brings trained, verified Namamians to people's homes for house, car and appliance cleaning, in Bhopal, Vidisha, Sagar, Chhatarpur and Nagpur. Everything we make should feel like the visit itself: calm, careful and on time.

Namamicleans brand guidelinesBrand · 04

Voice and tone

Write the way a good Namamian talks at the door: polite, brief, and clear about what happens next.

Sound like a person, not a system.Your visit is booked for Tue, 12 Nov, 10:00 am.Your booking has been successfully processed.
Short and specific.Ravi will reach by 10:00 am.Our professional will arrive within the scheduled time window!!
Errors say what happened and what to do.That payment didn't go through. Try again, or pay by UPI.Error 402: Transaction failed.
Invite, don't pressure.2 slots left on Saturday morning.Hurry!! Only 2 slots left, book NOW!
Hindi comes first in the Namamian app.काम शुरू करें · सुबह 9:00Start job · 09:00 AM

Writing mechanics

Our name. Namamicleans, one word, capital N. Never Namami Cleans, NamamiCleans or NAMAMICLEANS.
Our people. Field staff are Namamians, never "captain", "worker" or "maid". Use their name where we can: "Ravi from Namamicleans".
Case. Sentence case for headings, buttons and menus.
Numbers. Numerals, Indian grouping, rupee sign: ₹1,499 · ₹1,23,456. Times as 10:00 am; in Hindi, सुबह/दोपहर/शाम/रात 10:00, never AM/PM.
Buttons. Say what happens: Book visit, Pay ₹1,499. The result echoes it: Visit booked.
Hindi glossary for booking status, used everywhere:

The drop

One master drawing, two colours. It holds up from a 16 px browser tab to a shop sign.

Two partsThe body (Namami Teal) and the curl (Gold). Every colourway recolours these two parts, nothing else.
Proportions2072 wide by 2704 tall: always this shape, never squashed to fit a box.
Never redraw itAlways place the master file from the brand kit. Don't trace, rebuild, simplify or adjust it by eye.
Small sizesBelow 20 px, use the favicon files: they give the drop more of the tile so it still reads.

Namamicleans brand guidelinesLogo · 06

Lockups

Four approved arrangements, all generated from the same two masters. Use the horizontal lockup unless the space clearly asks for another.

Horizontal: the drop is 1.62 × H tall and sits 0.46 × H from the name. Stacked: the drop is 3.1 × H tall, 0.62 × H above the name. H is the height of the capital N.

Gold is only ever on Namami Teal. On white and light grounds the whole logo is teal, because gold on white is too faint to read (1.6:1).

Namamicleans brand guidelinesOur two apps · 14

App icons and the Namamian app

One drop, one recipe, two colourways. Customers see the drop on white. Our Namamians' app is the same drop reversed on Namami Teal, so the two never get mixed up on a phone.

Same size everywherePhone icon: drop is 44% of Android's 108 dp canvas (66% of what the launcher shows). Play, iOS and app-store squares: 62%. Browser tabs: 70%, so it still reads at 16 px.
Never put the name in an iconThe drop alone. The store and the phone already show the name.
Generated, not drawnEvery icon file comes from the kit's generator. If a size is missing, add it to the generator; never export by hand.

Namamicleans brand guidelinesStandards · 17

Do's and don'ts

The short version of everything in this book. When in doubt, choose the simpler option.

DoDon't
NameWrite Namamicleans, one word, capital N.Write Namami Cleans, NAMAMICLEANS, or set the name in another font.
LogoUse the kit's master files with one H of clear space.Redraw, stretch, recolour, or put the gold logo on white.
ColourLet white, grey and ink carry the page. Action Teal for what people tap.Flood every surface with teal, or use gold for text and icons.
TypePoppins for headings, Mukta for text and Hindi, sizes from the tokens.Add other typefaces or set headings in all caps.
ImageryReal Namamians and real homes, in natural light, with consent.Stock smiles, heavy filters, or AI images presented as our work.
VoiceShort, polite, specific. Hindi first for Namamians.Shout, stack exclamation marks, or hide behind jargon.
Digital48 px targets (56 px for Namamians), visible focus, alt text that says Namamicleans.Signal status with colour alone, or autoplay motion.
Namamicleans brand guidelinesStandards · 18

Standards

How Namamicleans behaves around claims, people and partners.

Honest claimsNo "best in Bhopal" or "No. 1" without a source we can show. Prices say what they include. Before-and-after photos are real and from the same job.
PrivacyCustomers' homes and faces never appear without written consent. We follow the DPDP Act, 2023: see namamicleans.com/privacy-policy.
AccessEvery screen meets WCAG 2.1 AA. Plain words, in Hindi and English, so everyone can book and every Namamian can work.
LicensingPoppins and Mukta are free under the SIL Open Font License. Every photo is licensed or our own, and credited where needed.
TrademarkUse ™ after Namamicleans in formal contexts until registration completes, then ®. Confirm with trademark counsel first.
File namingnamamicleans-[lockup]-[colourway]@[width]w.pngmatching the kit, so the right file is always findable.
